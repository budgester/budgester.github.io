Every company now uses software, and a lot of them develop software.

A lot of time is spent on getting software built, tested and deployed, and many of the steps teams and companies go through are similar. So why reinvent the wheel every time?

DevOps is a way of working, and of getting the best out of the tools that are available for building software.

## What are the steps to making this simple?

If you do something more than once, automate it.

But start simple, move one step at a time, and get quicker with each iteration.

## Some of my favourite books on DevOps and software management

- *Continuous Delivery*
- *The Phoenix Project*
- *The DevOps Handbook*
- *Release It!*
