In January 2018 I crewed *Challenger 2* from Antigua back to the UK with the Tall Ships Youth Trust, booked through First Class Sailing. She's one of the 72 ft steel yachts built for the BT Global Challenge "wrong way" round-the-world race. We left Jolly Harbour on 8 January, stopped in Horta in the Azores, made landfall at Falmouth at the end of January, and finished up the Channel to Portsmouth. I was on the Amazons watch.

The skipper, the mate and the crew sent a blog home from the boat. These are all the posts from the voyage, on the First Class Sailing site.
